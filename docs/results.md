## How to read these numbers

1. **The model's score is inflated by the dataset.** 97.8% of PaySim frauds move exactly the sender's whole balance, and no genuine payment does. Without the two sender-balance features, PR-AUC falls from 0.985 to 0.581. Real fraud is not this clean.
2. **The model cannot see scams.** PaySim has no authorised-scam labels and no customer history. Scam-shaped payments reach the chat through a behaviour rule (a large first transfer to a new payee) that relies on synthetic customer profiles.
3. **The chat evaluation is small and friendly.** 20 conversations written by the same author as the agent's instructions, with scripted customers. Treat it as evidence that the mechanism works, not as an accuracy estimate for real customers.
4. **The two layers were evaluated separately.** There is no end-to-end figure for how much real scam loss the combined system would prevent.
